I have been fortunate to have been in the leadership team founding 2 Digital Bank Ventures. These are the 12 things I learned from this experience:

### 1) Reaching the goal is not a straight line.

Be prepared for lots of bumps and challenges along the way.

![Reaching the goal is not a straight line](/images/blog/a1.png)

### 2) Launch MVP (Minimal Viable Product) to find Product-Market fit

Is this product really solving problems for customers?

![Launch MVP](/images/blog/a2.png)

### 3) The Risk of the Product reduces the closer it gets to shipment to customers.

The more opportunity you have to iterate a version of the product with real customers the better the outcome.

![Risk reduces closer to shipment](/images/blog/a3.png)

### 4) Don't go overboard with Agile!

You need to have a high-level plan which roughly lays down the key milestones and then let the agile teams work through the details.

![Don't go overboard with Agile](/images/blog/a4.png)

### 5) Small Motivated Teams are better than large complex teams.

2 Pizza team of 8 people is a good benchmark.

![Small motivated teams](/images/blog/a5.png)

### 6) Manage dependencies between different teams as they grow.

Important to keep the focus on what each team is doing and what their part is in slicing the elephant.

![Manage dependencies](/images/blog/a6.png)

### 7) Develop a Test & Learn approach to building the product.

Don't assume you know what customers want and what works. Iterate through the prototype, get feedback & improve.

![Test and learn](/images/blog/a7-1.png)

### 8) Test Product-Market fit with users.

Make this a data & metric driven process, not just something based on gut feel. e.g. what % of users would be disappointed if the product is removed from the app store.

![Test product-market fit](/images/blog/a8.png)

### 9) Release the updates to the product often

To make it a simple process and build the muscle to deploy quickly.

![Release often](/images/blog/a9.png)

### 10) Alignment across teams and stakeholders is not that obvious

![Alignment across teams](/images/blog/a10.png)

### 11) Take time to be more effective.

Not be too busy to improve the productivity of what you are doing as you build.

![Take time to be more effective](/images/blog/a11.png)

### 12) Last but not the least... Work Hard, Have fun, Make history!!!

![Work hard, have fun, make history](/images/blog/a12-1.png)
