# Personal Brand Site — Ideas

## High Impact

### 1. Newsletter / Email Capture
- Zero owned-audience infrastructure right now
- Add email signup (ConvertKit, Buttondown, or Resend) to homepage, blog posts, and a dedicated `/subscribe` page
- Social followers are rented; an email list is owned
- Placement: homepage hero or about teaser, end of every blog post, dedicated page

### 2. Speaking / Media Page
- `/speaking` page with past appearances, podcasts, panels, keynotes
- List topics available for (digital banking, crypto regulation, AI, venture building)
- Include a booking CTA or calendar link
- Turns passive interest into inbound speaking requests

### 3. Photo / Headshot
- No image of Saurav anywhere on the site — personal brands need a face
- Add professional headshot to about page hero and homepage about teaser
- Consider a smaller avatar in the site header or footer

### 4. Social Proof Section
- Logo bar on homepage: Standard Chartered, Citibank, TRUST Bank, Mox Bank, Oxford, NUS
- Press mentions or media logos if available
- Builds instant credibility with first-time visitors

## Medium Impact

### 5. Ventures / Case Studies Page
- `/ventures` page with richer detail on TRUST, Mox, and Yirifi
- Structure per venture: the problem, your role, approach, outcomes, key metrics
- More compelling than compressed timeline bullet points
- Could include screenshots or product visuals

### 6. Blog Content + Consistent Cadence
- Currently 5 posts across 4 categories — too thin
- Pick a weekly or biweekly cadence and commit
- "Weekly Recap" category has 1 post — either commit to the series or drop the category
- Consistent publishing is the engine that makes everything else work

### 7. RSS Feed
- Astro makes this trivial with `@astrojs/rss`
- Lets people subscribe via feed readers
- Enables content syndication to other platforms

### 8. Open Graph Images
- Custom OG images per page for polished social sharing on LinkedIn/X
- Can generate dynamically with `@astrojs/og` or Satori
- Ensures links don't look generic when shared

## Lower Priority

### 9. Skills / Expertise Tags on About Page
- Resume lists 17+ skills not surfaced on the site
- Scannable grid of expertise areas: Digital Assets, CBDC, Tokenization, Product Strategy, etc.
- Helps visitors quickly understand what you bring

### 10. Testimonials / Endorsements
- 2-3 quotes from colleagues, board members, or partners
- Adds credibility that self-written bios can't provide
- Place on about page or homepage

### 11. Advisory / Board Roles
- If holding any advisory or board positions, list them
- Signals ongoing relevance beyond primary role
- Could be a small section on the about page
